## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. I am a complete beginner in college. Can I join the AIML Club?
**Yes!** We actively design our curriculum for first-year and beginner students. Start by reading [**learning_resources/00-roadmap/beginner-roadmap.md**](https://github.com/AIMLCLUBOCT/learning_resources/blob/main/00-roadmap/beginner-roadmap.md) and join our WhatsApp group.

### 2. How do I contribute my first project to the organization?
Read our [Global Contributing Guide](./CONTRIBUTING.md) and check out the [Projects repository](https://github.com/AIMLCLUBOCT/Projects). You can submit a project proposal issue or follow the project submission template.

### 3. How do I access workshop notebooks and slides?
All workshop code, datasets, and presentation materials are open-sourced in the [Workshops repository](https://github.com/AIMLCLUBOCT/Workshops).

### 4. Where can I find live updates about events?
Check out [live.aimlcluboct.in](https://live.aimlcluboct.in) or our [Events repository](https://github.com/AIMLCLUBOCT/EVENTS).

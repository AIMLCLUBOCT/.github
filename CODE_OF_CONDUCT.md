# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors, and leaders of the **AI & Machine Learning Club – Oriental College of Technology (OCT), Bhopal**, pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free, welcoming, and empowering experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy academic and technical community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other students and contributors.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and levels of technical experience.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall learning and growth of the club.
- Mentoring beginner peers and encouraging first-time contributors.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, intimidation, or bullying.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, contact numbers, or student identifiers, without their explicit permission.
- Plagiarism, submitting stolen academic work, or misrepresenting others' code as your own.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional and academic setting.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The AIML Club OCT Leadership Team and faculty advisors are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (including GitHub repositories, workshops, hackathons, lab sessions, WhatsApp groups, and online platforms), and also applies when an individual is officially representing the AIML Club OCT in public spaces or college events.

---

## Reporting Guidelines

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the leadership team at:

- 📧 **Email:** [aimlcluboct@gmail.com](mailto:aimlcluboct@gmail.com)
- 🗣️ **Anonymous Feedback Portal:** [voice.aimlcluboct.in](https://voice.aimlcluboct.in)

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The leadership team is obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
